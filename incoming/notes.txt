SeretGram 0.2 — a big update

- Film posters: each film shows the picture its group posted with it
- Search sits at the top of Home, on the TV and the phone; Photos has its own tab
- Your licence is entered once and kept through every update
- Lighter with big libraries: lists refresh calmly while the library loads
- Photos stay out of the way on Home and in Search unless you turn them on (Settings → Photos)
- Films posted under a Hebrew and an English name are one film
- Descriptions and categories from Wikipedia and Wikidata, no account needed
- Player: volume and brightness at their own edge; tap the sides to skip 10 s
- Updates: the app tells you when a new version is out and installs it in place
- 3 days free, then a one-time licence at seretgram.com
