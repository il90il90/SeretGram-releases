SeretGram — first release

- Your Telegram films and series in one library, on TV and phone
- Updates: the app tells you when a new version is out and installs it in place
- Films posted under a Hebrew and an English name are one film
- Categories from the posts themselves and Wikidata; descriptions from Wikipedia
- Lighter with big libraries: lists refresh calmly while the library loads
- Photos stay out of the way unless you turn them on (Settings → Photos)
- Player: volume and brightness at their own edge; tap the sides to skip 10 s
- Subtitles from Telegram or online, Hebrew and English
- 3 days free, then a one-time licence at seretgram.com
